# Nineweave Privacy Policy

**Effective date: November 15, 2026**  
**Owner: Patrick Booth**  
**Contact: [contact email]**

This Privacy Policy explains what information Nineweave collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. It applies to the website at nineweave.grok.me, the online game server, and related apps (together, the "Game").

## 1. Who we are

Nineweave is owned and operated by Patrick Booth. For privacy questions, requests to delete your data, or other requests about your information, contact [contact email].

## 2. Information we collect

We aim to collect only what we need to run the Game.

**Anonymous device accounts.** When you create a screen name for online play, we create an anonymous device account and store a secret token on your device. We store only a one-way hash of that token on the server, not the token itself. We do not require your real name, email address, or password to play.

**Screen names.** You choose a screen name. Screen names are public. Other players can see them in the lobby, in matches, and on the leaderboard.

**Gameplay records and the leaderboard.** We store your last-used character, how many full online matches you have played, won, and lost, and an internal count of small boards won. The official public score is matches won. The public leaderboard shows screen names, ranks, and match wins. The internal small-board count is not shown on the public board.

**Purchases and unlocks.** We may store a list of items you have purchased or unlocked so the Game can remember them. On iOS, optional real-money items may be offered through Apple In-App Purchase. On the web, optional items, if offered, are points-only. Until a purchase is independently verified, a value reported by the Game client is not treated as proof of payment.

**Consent records.** When you create a screen name, we store that you agreed to the Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy, the version of each document you agreed to, the time you agreed, and that you confirmed you are 13 or older or have a parent or guardian's permission.

**Technical information.** Our hosts may automatically receive standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, and request times, which they use to operate, secure, and diagnose the service. We do not use this to advertise to you.

We do not ask for your phone number, home address, or government ID. We do not have to collect more personal information than this to run online play.

## 3. How we use information

We use this information to:

- Let you play online, rejoin with the same device account, and keep your chosen character
- Run the lobby, matches, rematches, and the public leaderboard
- Remember purchases and unlocks
- Enforce our name rules and Terms of Service
- Keep the Game secure, fix problems, and understand how the service is doing
- Respond to your requests, including requests to delete your data

## 4. We do not sell your data

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with third parties for their own advertising or marketing. We share information only with the service providers who host the Game, as described below, or when we believe we must do so to comply with the law, protect people's safety, or enforce our Terms.

## 5. Hosting

The Game's website and server are hosted on Vercel and Grok hosting. Player records are stored in a Neon database. Those providers process data on our behalf to keep the Game running. They have their own privacy and security practices.

## 6. Public information

Your screen name and official match-win record are public when you appear on the leaderboard or play a public match. Do not choose a screen name that includes personal information you do not want others to see.

## 7. Data deletion

You can ask us to delete your device account, screen name, and gameplay records by writing to [contact email] and telling us your screen name and any other details that help us find the right account. We will delete that information, or anonymize it if we must keep a limited record (for example, to resolve a dispute or meet a legal duty). We will try to handle requests promptly. Leaderboard history and match logs that already include your screen name may take a short time to update.

## 8. Children (COPPA-minded)

The Game is intended for players who are 13 or older, or who have a parent or guardian's permission. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without a parent or guardian's consent. We do not require a real name, email address, or other contact information to play. If you believe we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without parental consent, contact [contact email] and we will delete it.

If you are a parent or guardian and you permit a child to play, you are responsible for that use, including the screen name they choose.

## 9. How long we keep information

We keep your device account and gameplay records for as long as the account is used to play, and for a reasonable period after that so you can return, unless you ask us to delete them sooner. We may delete unused accounts after a long period of inactivity. Technical logs kept by our hosts are generally retained only as long as needed for security and operations.

## 10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top will change when we do. If a change is significant, we will try to let you know in the Game or on the website. Continued use of the Game after an update means you accept the revised policy.

## 11. Contact

For privacy questions or to request deletion of your data, contact [contact email].
